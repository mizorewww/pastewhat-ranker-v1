# Train missing-intent replacement source gate

Snapshot: 2026-09-23, first Train replacement round, after 152 complete replacement batches. This is Train production evidence only; no Calibration or Test sample was inspected.

The original 2,000 registered Train batches completed with 19,066 accepted rows and 934 unfilled logical slots. Round 1 registered 676 immutable replacement batches for exactly those 934 unique slots. None overlap an originally accepted slot. In the first 101 attempted missing-intent replacement slots, only 29 were accepted (28.7%), across 34 conceptual families. The corresponding accepted counts were 84/99 for `no_match` and 19/21 for `select`. Standard-observation missing-intent plans accounted for 24/96 accepted slots. Rejection records for the unfilled missing-intent slots were dominated by independent labels choosing `select`; repeated author and label attempts mean rejection-event counts are not unique-slot counts.

At the observed 29/101 per-attempt rate, a deliberately simple stationary, independent model projects about 42.7 of the 456 original missing-intent slots still unfilled after all seven allowed replacement rounds. Its calculated all-filled probability is about 3.5e-20. That number is a risk illustration, not an estimate of the real process: attempts share prompts and families, and later authored situations may differ. Under the same model, the per-round acceptance rate would need to exceed 58.3% merely to bring the expected final unfilled count below one. The first-round evidence therefore justifies improving future source construction without changing labels or quotas.

For unregistered Train round 2 and later, batches containing a missing-intent plan receive `train-missing-intent-required-parameter-v1`. For a standard observation, the author must make a required decision parameter visible while leaving its value unspecified, and map distinct pasteable candidates to plausible alternative values. An author-only `decision_gate` records the visible requirement quote and value mapping. A pre-label gate checks that structure against the student-visible context; the author-only object is removed before preprocessing and independent labeling. For `no_accessibility` and `generic_field` observations, the gate instead requires empty guidance and selection, unchanged candidate count, and no decision metadata. The independent labeler remains authoritative and may still reject the draft.

Original Train sampling, round 1 replacement registration, frozen Pilot and Diagnostic snapshots, and all existing audit records remain immutable. The frozen Train stage independently replays the versioned author fixture and pre-label gate for any accepted round 2+ row. The new marker is inert for Dev and all existing Train sources.

## Early production check after the versioned source began

Snapshot: 2026-09-23 12:50 UTC, 108 completed round-2 missing-intent slots across 33 Train families. Of these, 100 were accepted (92.6%), compared with 29/101 (28.7%) in the earlier round-1 snapshot. At least 104 of the 108 reached independent labeling; 100 of those 104 ultimately passed the registered action and quality checks (96.2%). The pre-label gate recorded 12 structural-error events across 10 distinct slots, with repair attempts permitted. Six distinct slots had an independent-action mismatch event. Eight slots remained unfilled and will be offered to later registered rounds.

These are different sequential replacement cohorts, not a randomized experiment; the rate comparison is operational evidence that the new source is viable, not an isolated causal effect estimate or a real-world accuracy score. Independent labels were neither overwritten nor relabeled to satisfy a quota. The registered round-2 file contains 381 batches for 411 unique previously unfilled original slots; all 325 batches containing a missing-intent plan carry the new version marker, while the other 56 retain their ordinary source behavior.
